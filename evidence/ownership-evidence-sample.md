## Responsibility Summary

The Business Owner is accountable for the approved business use of the assistant, including whether the system should remain in pilot status or be expanded.

The Technical Owner is accountable for system configuration, access restrictions, logging, and technical implementation of control requirements.

The Data Owner is accountable for approved data sources, source-document currency, and classification of operational information used by the assistant.
